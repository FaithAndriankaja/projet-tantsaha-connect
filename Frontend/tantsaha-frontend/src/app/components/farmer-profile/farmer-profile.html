Our Story

For three generations, our family has cultivated the volcanic soils of Antsirabe. At Rakoto's Farm, we believe that the health of the community starts with the health of the soil. We've moved away from industrial fertilizers to embrace traditional Malagasy "Vandana" composting and natural pest management.

By integrating livestock with our crop cycles, we ensure a sustainable ecosystem that provides nutrient-dense produce while preserving the biodiversity of our beautiful highland region. Every harvest is a promise of quality and respect for our ancestors' legacy.
